Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

The Chip Confesses uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chip Confesses channels. It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

It does not offer sign-in to anyone else, and it collects, stores or shares no data about other people. The only access tokens it holds are the owner's own, and they are stored on the owner's computer.

Videos are placed on this site briefly so that Instagram can fetch them while publishing, then removed.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/aryanorastar/chipconfesses.
